    Getting started

    New here?

    1. Select cells, then a preset or a format cycle (Ctrl+Shift+1, 2, 4 to 7).

    2. Links: export a range, a chart or one cell to PowerPoint and update it later.

    3. Brand colors and Custom styles sit below Model formatting: your palette in one click, and your own cell styles.

    4. Every section has a ? beside its heading.

    • Undo: Excel's Ctrl+Z cannot take back a pls,fix button. Press Ctrl+Shift+Z or Undo last pls,fix action (Tools > Model tools).

      Changed the source? Press Copy all (or tick links and Copy selected), then paste in PowerPoint > Inbox. The paste updates the slides.

      How links reach PowerPoint

      Transport

      Nothing leaves this computer: export or copy here, then paste in PowerPoint's pls,fix pane, Inbox tab.

      Pushes go to the relay beside this pane; PowerPoint updates with one click once the link key is pasted there.
